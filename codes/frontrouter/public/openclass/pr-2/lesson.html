Lesson 1: What is a cell?

A cell is the smallest unit of life. Your body is made of trillions of cells, and each one carries out the work that keeps you alive.

The three main parts

Membrane
The thin outer layer that controls what goes in and out.
Cytoplasm
The jelly-like fluid where most of the work happens.
Nucleus
The control centre that holds the genetic instructions.
